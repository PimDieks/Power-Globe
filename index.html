An interactive globe showing the weather forecast. Drag to turn it, pinch or scroll to zoom, and tap a spot to see its forecast. Search for an airport to see its ground conditions and the wind on each runway, or open the wind check for a list of airports where wind and gusts are near or over the limits. Pick a time on the timeline. Live flights, military flights and satellites can be switched on under Live traffic; tap one for its details. Zoomed in, runways are coloured by whether they can be used in the wind.
